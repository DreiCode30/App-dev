# My Favorite Series: Dexter

**Dexter** is a crime drama series about *Dexter Morgan*, a blood spatter analyst for the Miami Metro Police Department who secretly lives a double life as a serial killer. He follows a strict moral code, taught to him by his adoptive father Harry, that only allows him to target people who escape justice.

## Why I Love It

- Dexter's dark inner narration
- A clever mix of suspense, dark humor, and drama
- Memorable villains in every season
- A complex main character who makes you question right and wrong

> "Tonight's the night." (a recurring line in the show)
